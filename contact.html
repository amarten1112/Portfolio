FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What's your typical project timeline?

Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity. Small web design projects typically take 2-4 weeks, while full-stack development projects range from 6-12 weeks. We'll establish clear milestones during the discovery phase.

Do you work with agencies or only direct clients?

I work with both! I've partnered with creative agencies on larger projects and also take on direct client work. I'm flexible with collaboration structures.

What's your design process?

I follow a collaborative, iterative approach: discovery and research, strategy and ideation, design and prototyping, testing and refinement, and final implementation. You're involved at every stage.

Do you do custom development or just web design?

I specialize in both! I can handle complete end-to-end projects from design through full-stack development, or I can work as a specialist in either area depending on your needs.

What's your tech stack?

For web development, I typically work with React, Node.js, PostgreSQL, and modern JavaScript. I'm comfortable with other technologies based on project requirements and am always learning new tools.

Can you help with existing projects?

Absolutely! I take on projects to redesign websites, refactor code, improve performance, or add new features to existing applications. Let's discuss what you need.
